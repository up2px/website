# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Very early-stage startups & indie builders:** Founders with tight budgets who need a sharp, credible online presence, MVP landing page, or frontend without bloated agency overhead.
2. **Local businesses & service owners (gyms, cafes, studios, boutiques):** Business owners who need clean, fast websites, product/service catalogs, or direct lead capture (e.g. WhatsApp CTAs) to start driving real local customers.

*Crucial user perception:* The agency must feel accessible and high-value, never "cheap" or desperate. Pricing is approachable and transparent, but the craft, performance, and attention to detail reflect high-standard modern engineering.

## Product Purpose

up2px is a nimble digital design and build studio. The website exists to showcase the studio's craft, establish immediate trust without needing a decade-long portfolio, communicate clear service offerings, and prompt visitors to start a project conversation.

## Positioning

**High-craft, high-performance web experiences built directly with the founders, without the agency markup.**
- **Direct access:** Clients work directly with the builders/designers (no account managers or bureaucracy).
- **Cost-effective without compromise:** Modern, conversion-ready sites accessible to early startups and local businesses.
- **Craft & Performance:** Instant load times, responsive fluid layouts, and intentional micro-interactions that make even simple sites feel bespoke and polished.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Services Offered:**
  - High-converting Landing Pages (for startups, launches, apps).
  - Catalog & Showcase Sites with direct WhatsApp / direct-contact CTAs (for local businesses like cafes, gyms, creators).
  - Full E-commerce & Storefronts.
  - Bespoke Design Systems & Brand Kits.
  - Design-to-Code sprints (Figma to Next.js/Tailwind).
- **Operating Context & Stack:**
  - Framework: Next.js (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS.
  - Contact Mechanism: An elegant, interactive project inquiry modal/form that delivers instant notifications to the team via Discord webhook in the background, appearing to clients as a streamlined, responsive project intake system.
- **Constraints:**
  - Honest proof: Since the studio is early, avoid fake client logos, fabricated metrics, or stock corporate testimonials. Instead, showcase interactive live demos, design component previews, and transparent process breakdowns.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** **up2px** (written lowercase).
- **Aesthetic Direction:**
  - Hybrid modern studio craft: clean, hyper-legible body typography and spacious layout paired with tasteful Nothing-inspired dot-matrix / pixel accents, hairline grid accents, and subtle glassmorphic surfaces.
  - The main text prioritizes effortless readability over stylistic extremes (no illegible display fonts for copy).
  - Palette: Deep dark grounding (`#0A0A0A` / `#121212`), crisp high-contrast text (`#F4F4F0`), hairline grid borders, and dynamic signal accents (`#FF3B00` / warm orange-red).
  - Micro-interactions: Tactile feedback, subtle hover states, smooth transitions, and deliberate interaction polish.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet (new agency). Proof is demonstrated through the quality of the site itself, interactive component showcases, clear deliverables, and upfront execution guarantees.

## Product Principles

1. **The craft is the proof:** When you lack a 10-year case study deck, your own website, speed, and interactions do the selling.
2. **Accessible, never cheap:** Accessible pricing meets uncompromising visual refinement. We speak directly about value and outcomes.
3. **Readable first, stylized with purpose:** Aesthetic accents (dot-matrix, subtle glassmorphism) frame and elevate the content without hindering readability.
4. **Frictionless conversation:** One polished, low-friction project inquiry pathway that feels fast, personal, and respectful of the client's time.
