# Surface brief: landing page (`app/page.tsx`)

Scope: The up2px agency landing page. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Build path: code-led.

Audience and action: 
- Early-stage founders & indie makers seeking high-converting MVP/launch sites.
- Local business owners (gyms, cafes, boutiques) needing clean catalog/showcase sites with direct inquiry channels (WhatsApp, instant forms).
- Primary action: Start a project / request an intake inquiry (front-facing sleek project modal/form that pings direct Discord webhook notifications internally).

Proof: No fabricated client logos or fake stats. Proof is demonstrated through interactive UI/micro-interaction demos, live site speed, transparent scope breakdowns, and clear deliverables.

Working process: Step-by-step modular development with real-time preview and alignment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern high-craft design and engineering studio website that balances hyper-readable, elegant structure with tactile micro-interactions, subtle glassmorphic depth, and signature pixel/dot-matrix accent details. It communicates premium quality without appearing inaccessible or bloated.

OWN-WORLD:
- Base Ground: `#0A0A0A` / `#0D0D0D` with subtle frosted translucent glass layers (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/[0.03]` or `bg-neutral-900/60`).
- Typography: Clean, legible sans for headings and long-form copy (Inter/Host Grotesk/Geist) ensuring high contrast and effortless legibility; Doto / Geist Mono reserved for technical data tags, badges, and dot-matrix visual accents.
- Accent & Signals: Precision `#FF3B00` signal orange for key focal points, badges, and action triggers.
- Geometry & Layout: Ruled hairline grid framing (`border-white/10` or `border-neutral-800`), crosshair details, and responsive card layouts that feel grounded rather than floaty.

NARRATIVE FLOW:
1. **Header:** Brand mark, services navigation, process, contact button, subtle theme/accent state.
2. **Hero:** Clear, punchy value proposition ("High-craft websites for early startups and ambitious local businesses"), direct outcome subheadline, dual action (Start a Project / Explore Work & Solutions), and interactive showcase.
3. **What We Build (Services & Solutions):**
   - High-Converting Landing Pages (MVPs, SaaS, waitlists).
   - Fast Local Showcase & Catalog Sites (gyms, cafes, studios, WhatsApp-connected).
   - E-commerce & Storefronts.
   - Design Systems & Design-to-Code sprints.
4. **Interactive Craft Showcase / Demo:** Interactive UI components and micro-interactions demonstrating the studio's technical execution.
5. **How We Work (Process):** Direct founder collaboration, rapid turnaround, no agency bloat.
6. **Transparent Scope & Approach:** Honest pricing tiers/ranges and deliverables.
7. **Interactive Project Intake Modal / Form:** Sleek, frictionless form (project type, timeline, contact details) connected to an internal webhook.
8. **Footer:** Studio brand, quick links, status indicator ("Available for new projects").

FINISH: Full responsive pass, accessibility review, tactile hover/interaction refinement, and cohesive aesthetic hierarchy.
